Supporting calculations

    Interpretation

    What this tool does

    Review a batch of paired results without treating nondetects as zero or applying one percent criterion at every concentration.

    When to use it

    For already paired laboratory or field duplicates with matching methods, units and reporting bases. Use the same criterion only for an appropriate analyte/method group; run separate batches when limits differ.

    Inputs and supported units

    Use the labelled laboratory units and a consistent sample basis. Values are not silently interpreted as a regulatory method. Numeric input changes clear earlier results; CSV files are read locally, without uploading. The demonstration values only illustrate the arithmetic.

    Calculation or decision method

    For two uncensored concentrations with positive mean, RPD = 100 × |a−b| / ((a+b)/2). If either result is below your entered RL multiplier, compare absolute difference with your entered absolute limit instead. Equality to the limit meets that arithmetic comparison.

    If either result is written as <number, the tool retains it and marks the pair not assessed; it makes no zero, half-RL or full-RL substitution. Two exact zeros have undefined RPD and can only be assessed by an enabled absolute-difference policy. No automatic qualifier or batch-acceptance flag is assigned.

    How to interpret the result

    Read the breakdown, exclusions and input record before the headline. Meeting an entered numeric condition is not a complete analytical quality decision. Retain the original bench sheet and resolve differences with the laboratory reviewer.

    Worked example

    10 and 12 mg/L give an absolute difference of 2 mg/L and an RPD of 18.1818%. For 0.05 and 0.06 at RL 0.02, the demonstration multiplier 5 selects an absolute difference of 0.01 instead. A result written <0.02 is preserved as censored and receives no numeric precision verdict.

    Assumptions

    The entered measurements describe the same stated sample, method and basis. Inputs must already account for any upstream dilution not explicitly modeled. Criteria come from the governing SOP, not from this website.

    Limitations

    RPD evaluates duplicate precision, not analytical accuracy or recovery. Do not use percent differences for logarithmic pH values or unmatched units, methods, dilution bases or sample identities. The project's real low-level/nondetect rules may differ; review excluded pairs under those rules rather than editing censor symbols to force a number. Repeated results can agree and still share the same bias.

    Laboratory and safety boundary. Numeric review only. The approved analytical method, laboratory SOP and qualified analyst control testing, acceptance, reporting and follow-up. No result establishes potable safety or regulatory compliance. These tools do not instruct sample preparation, chemical handling, incubation, furnace work or laboratory waste disposal.
