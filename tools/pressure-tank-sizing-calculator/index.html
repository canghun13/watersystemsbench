Supporting calculations

    Interpretation

    What this tool does

    Convert an entered minimum pump run time into required drawdown and idealized nominal tank volume. Optionally check a manufacturer-rated drawdown against that requirement without confusing usable water with the tank's nominal capacity.

    When to use it

    Use for preliminary conventional on/off pump systems, not VFD/constant-pressure controls or thermal-expansion vessels. Obtain the pump's delivery at the relevant pressure and the pump/motor manufacturer's run-time requirement; the example's 1 minute is not a universal recommendation.

    Inputs and supported units

    Each labelled field explains the quantity it expects. Unit-system changes convert existing numeric entries once and keep calculations on a single SI internal basis. Supported shared units include L/min and GPM, metres and feet, kPa and psi, millimetres and inches, kW and hp, and °C and °F as relevant.

    Calculation or decision method

    Required drawdown = pump flow × entered minimum run time
    Drawdown fraction = P₀(abs) × [1/Pin(abs) − 1/Pout(abs)]

    Gauge pressures are converted to absolute by adding atmospheric pressure. The tool requires precharge < cut-in < cut-out. The original ideal-gas sizing model is unchanged.

    Compare rated drawdown, not nominal capacity

    1. Establish the requirement. Use pump delivery at the relevant duty/pressure, not a household fixture count, motor horsepower alone or unrestricted faucet flow. A changing pump curve needs a manufacturer-reviewed sizing flow or conservative operating cases; this tool does not model the full curve.
    2. Read the correct rating. Find usable drawdown between the actual cut-out and cut-in pressures in the current tank data. Check precharge, tank configuration and other stated rating conditions. A catalogue's nominal litres or gallons are not drawdown.
    3. Confirm the basis before comparing. Choose the manufacturer-rated comparison, enter its drawdown and confirm the rating conditions match. If conditions differ, leave the comparison unconfirmed and obtain the correct rating; do not interpolate a product table or change precharge to force a favorable result.
    4. Review the margin. Rated drawdown minus required drawdown gives a surplus or shortfall. Equality meets only the entered arithmetic target. Pressure/temperature suitability, relief protection, motor start limits and final selection still require the applicable manufacturer instructions and qualified review.
    Drawdown margin = entered rated drawdown − required drawdown
    Coverage (%) = 100 × entered rated drawdown / required drawdown
    No-demand refill-time estimate = entered rated drawdown / entered pump flow

    The refill estimate assumes constant inflow and no simultaneous outlet demand. It is not a guaranteed minimum actual run time, a starts-per-hour analysis, or a diagnosis of a waterlogged tank. Use the existing Short-Cycling Analyzer for a separate demand/cycle screen.

    How to interpret the result

    Read the primary result with the supporting breakdown and warning. A plausible number is not evidence that every input, operating case or design constraint has been included.

    Worked example

    A 40 L/min pump with an illustrative 1-minute target needs 40 L drawdown. At 193 kPa precharge and 207/345 kPa cut-in/out, the idealized fraction is about 29.5%, giving 135.5 L nominal volume. A hypothetical tank rated for only 32 L drawdown at those verified conditions is 8 L short (80% coverage), with a no-demand refill estimate of 0.8 minutes. Another hypothetical rating of 50 L gives a 10 L surplus (125%) and 1.25 minutes. These are demonstration ratings, not product recommendations; neither a 136 L label nor an unchecked pressure rating proves suitability.

    Assumptions

    Ideal-gas behavior, fixed atmospheric pressure and a conventional captive-air tank without thermal or bladder-volume corrections. Optional drawdown is entered from current manufacturer data at matching conditions; the user's confirmation is not independent verification. Pump inflow is treated as constant only for the no-demand refill estimate.

    Limitations

    No automatic model selection, product database, VFD sizing, thermal-expansion sizing or pressure-vessel approval. The tool does not verify the supplied rating, prescribe precharge or run time, model varying pump delivery, account for outlet demand during refill, or replace motor start limits and current manufacturer instructions.

    Safety and review boundary. A pressure tank is a sealed pressure vessel. Isolate power and fully release water pressure before checking precharge; follow the tank and pump instructions.
