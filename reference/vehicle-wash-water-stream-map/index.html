Vehicle Wash Water Stream Map

Use this map to keep unlike water streams and measurement points separate. It does not classify wastewater, select treatment or approve reuse or discharge.

Values are provided for transparent preliminary work. Confirm source conditions, product data and project-specific requirements.

Measurement record

  • Meter IDs, units, multipliers, start/end readings and exact interval.
  • Vehicles processed, wash packages, test washes and downtime.
  • Fresh, reclaim and permeate production or delivery measurements.
  • Tank working levels, reserve and overflow or bypass events.
  • RO feed, permeate, reject and operating hours at actual conditions.
  • Backwash, purge, cleaning and sludge-handling events.
  • Current tariff, sewer method, equipment data and authority checks.
A loop is not lossless. Carryout, evaporation, reject, backwash, purge, leaks and maintenance prevent 100% recovery. Never count the same returned volume as both reclaim and avoided discharge without a complete boundary.
Reference boundary. Do not convert an indicative value into a universal design, health or legal requirement. Check the cited source, laboratory context, certified claim and applicable jurisdiction.
