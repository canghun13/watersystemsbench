Water Laboratory QC Calculation Bases

Use the right denominator, units and evidence. The same-looking percentage can describe a different property or require a different concentration basis.

Numeric review only. The approved analytical method, laboratory SOP and qualified analyst control testing, acceptance, reporting and follow-up. No result establishes potable safety or regulatory compliance.

Direct seed and seeded water are not interchangeable

Direct seed uses the seed-suspension quantity in each bottle. Seeded dilution water uses the fraction occupied by that water, with no additional direct seed in this model. Document the full control-bottle volume and suspension identity. Unseeded blank depletion is a QC check, not a generic term to subtract from every bottle.

Precision and recovery are different percentages

RPD uses the mean of two uncensored paired observations as the denominator. Recovery uses the known analyte addition on a matched basis. A signed percent change against an original value is neither the absolute RPD used here nor a matrix-spike recovery.

Not assessed is an honest output

Zero-mean RPD is undefined. Censored values are inequalities, not measured zeros. No qualifying seed controls means the seeded result lacks a usable correction. Zero dry residue has no defined volatile percentage. Keep these conditions visible for method-specific review.

Mass and volume checklist

  • 1 g =1000 mg;1 L =1000 mL.
  • For net residue in mg and sample in mL, concentration in mg/L is mass×1000/volume.
  • Confirm whether sample volume is original or already diluted.
  • Confirm whether added spike volume and native dilution are represented in the reported concentration.
  • A user-entered minimum captured mass is not a universally valid reporting limit.

Scope of the calculation

The tools do not contain method-specific holding times, calibration tolerances, universal GGA/blank limits, jurisdictional reporting tables, automatic data qualifiers or treatment recommendations. Use the current approved method and the laboratory's review process.

Review boundary. Numeric review only. The approved analytical method, laboratory SOP and qualified analyst control testing, acceptance, reporting and follow-up. No result establishes potable safety or regulatory compliance.
