Groundwater Low-Flow Field Parameters

Field parameters support an operating record only when the instrument, units, timing, flow, water level and project-specific criterion remain attached to each reading.

Values are provided for transparent preliminary work. Confirm source conditions, product data and project-specific requirements.

Calculation and record rules

  • Use at least three consecutive, strictly time-ordered data rows.
  • Do not silently omit blank, malformed or inconvenient rows.
  • Keep criteria values and their governing source with the result.
  • Integrate purge volume from each time interval and its preceding measured flow.
  • Report each parameter as PASS or CHECK; one CHECK keeps the overall result at NOT YET MET.
  • Retain calibration, equipment, water-level, adjustment and interruption records outside the numeric table.
MET is deliberately narrow. It means only that the selected consecutive readings passed every user-entered numeric criterion. It does not establish representativeness, approve sample collection, specify laboratory handling or authorize purge-water disposal.
Reference boundary. Do not convert an indicative value into a universal design, health or legal requirement. Check the cited source, laboratory context, certified claim and applicable jurisdiction.
