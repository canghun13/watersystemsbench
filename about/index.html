About Water Systems Bench

An independent, English-language workflow hub for sizing, checking, troubleshooting and planning real-world water systems.

The project publishes transparent methods, assumptions and sources. It does not claim project-specific design, potable-safety or regulatory approval.

Why a bench?

Water decisions rarely fit one calculator. A field bench keeps measurements, assumptions, formulas and next steps together. The implemented scope connects Pumps, Pressure & Pipe Flow; Wells, Storage & Rainwater; Irrigation & Sprinkler Systems; Water Treatment & Water Quality; Greywater Reuse Planning; and Vehicle Wash Water Reclaim Planning.

Editorial approach

  • Static, readable content alongside interactive tools.
  • SI-first calculations with common US customary units.
  • Methods and limitations visible near every result.
  • Primary technical and public-agency sources where available.
  • No invented ratings, reviews, certifications or professional credentials.

Technology

The site uses static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript and is deployed with GitHub Pages and Cloudflare.
